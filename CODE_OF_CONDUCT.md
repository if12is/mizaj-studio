# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

- Be respectful and patient, in Arabic or English. Many contributors are new to open source.
- Critique code and ideas, not people. No harassment, insults or discriminatory language.
- Assume good faith and help newcomers find their way.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer through the contact on the
[maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/if12is). Reports are handled confidentially, and
the maintainer may remove comments or block participants who break these rules.
